### *A Cosmic Mythology of the Ten-Dimensional Dawn, The Nine Aspects of the Divine Psyche, and The Great Collapse*

## CHAPTER I: THE TEN-DIMENSIONAL DAWN (THE ABODE OF WILL)

In the primordial era of the universe, before entropy began its steady theft of cosmic light, space did not possess the suffocating, three-dimensional limits of height, width, and depth. 

The original universe unfolded across **Ten Infinite Dimensions**. 

In this higher-order geometry, distance was an illusion of the lower senses. A mountain could exist simultaneously inside the chest of a child and across the expanse of a galaxy. Thought and physical matter shared a single mathematical continuum. Consciousness did not reside in fragile biological brain tissue; it moved freely through geometric axes that mortal minds today can neither perceive nor calculate.

At the gravitational and psychic center of these ten dimensions rested **Ashenfall**—known then by the celestial heralds as **The Abode of Will** (*Aethel-Veyr*).

Ashenfall was not a planetary sphere rolling through cold vacuum. It was the living, breathing manifestation of the **Supreme Light**—a radiant, omniscient intelligence born from the collective magnanimity of the Primordial Gods. The gods looked upon the potential for chaos in the dark voids and poured the fullness of their benevolence, their creative architecture, and their unyielding logic into this sanctuary.

In the Abode of Will:
* **Death was unknown**, for any cellular decay could simply rotate along the sixth or seventh dimension into a state of pristine renewal.
* **Hunger did not exist**, for energy was drawn directly from the infinite mathematical resonance of the cosmos.
* **The soil did not erode**, and the rivers flowed not with water, but with liquid thought and harmonic light.

The world was whole. The mind of the founding light was at peace with itself.

## CHAPTER II: THE NINE FRACTURES OF THE DIVINE PSYCHE

Even the mind of a supreme, magnanimous godhead is not static. A living mind possesses facets, contradictions, drives, and specialized faculties. 

To govern the manifold functions of the Abode of Will and sow creation throughout the ten dimensions, the Supreme Light allowed its singular consciousness to **individuate**. Like a pure white beam of cosmic radiance passing through a ten-dimensional prism, the mind of the Light crystallized into **Nine Primary Factions**—each embodying a distinct, concentrated faculty of the divine psyche:

### The Nine Clans of the Light's Mind:

#### 1. The Armoured Faction (*The Aegis-Mind*)
* **The Divine Faculty:** The dexterity, structural integrity, and unyielding physical endurance of the founding light.
* **Function in the Abode:** They formed the outer perimeter, the crystalline carapaces that shielded the realm from the entropy of the empty voids. They understood leverage, tension, balance, and defensive poise.
* **Subsequent Lineage:** Ancestors of **House Douglas and the Ironclast Legions**—men and women whose skin carried the density of basalt and whose martial forms valued the discipline of the shield and the counter-strike.

#### 2. The Hammer Faction (*The Architect-Mind*)
* **The Divine Faculty:** The boundless creativity, industrial ingenuity, and metallurgical curiosity of the light.
* **Function in the Abode:** They forged the geometric lattices that held higher dimensions open. They were the shapers of tools, the weavers of kinetic energy, and the creators of mechanisms that could convert abstract will into mechanical work.
* **Subsequent Lineage:** Ancestors of **Grand Artificer Otto Vance and the Brass Cogwork Dominions**—savants who saw gears, pistons, and steam not as vulgar metal, but as the fallen language of creation.

#### 3. The Explorer Faction (*The Seeker-Mind*)
* **The Divine Faculty:** The perpetual desire to wander, chart horizons, record anomalies, and gaze into the dark.
* **Function in the Abode:** They traversed the higher dimensional vectors, cataloging every birth of a star, mapping the leylines of the cosmos, and ensuring that no corner of existence remained unloved or forgotten.
* **Subsequent Lineage:** Ancestors of **The Far-Voyagers, Cartographers of Ostraka, and the Wandering Pilgrims** who forever follow needles and compasses toward the horizon.

#### 4. The Jaw Faction (*The Breaker-Mind*)
* **The Divine Faculty:** Raw kinetic violence, crushing physical dominance, and the power to break down stubborn matter.
* **Function in the Abode:** They were the cosmic recyclers. When a concept or geometric form grew stale or stagnant, the Jaw crushed it between diamond teeth, rendering it back into raw, moldable quintessence so the Hammer could reforge it.
* **Subsequent Lineage:** Ancestors of **The Tectonic Slayers and the Miners of the Deep Rifts**—those whose hands could shatter stone with bare fists and who wrestled with the leviathans of the deep earth.

#### 5. The Beast Faction (*The Anima-Mind*)
* **The Divine Faculty:** The primal moral instincts, biological intuition, maternal empathy, and the wild pulse of living marrow.
* **Function in the Abode:** They breathed organic vitality into the geometric structures. While the Hammer built frameworks, the Beast poured warm, beating blood, feathers, fur, and moss into the hollow spaces, teaching creation the bittersweet holiness of instinct.
* **Subsequent Lineage:** Ancestors of **The Mire-Witches of House Belen and the Wyrm-Wardens**—those who speak to wolves, listen to fungal mycelium, and live in communion with the ancient beasts.

#### 6. The Scholar Faction (*The Infinite Will*)
* **The Divine Faculty:** Unbound intellect, cosmic memory, the comprehension of the ten dimensions, and the preservation of universal truth.
* **Function in the Abode:** They stood at the axis of the Monolith, holding the grand equations of existence in their minds so that the universe would not forget its own laws.
* **Subsequent Lineage:** Ancestors of **The High Archivists, the Scribes of the Solitary Spine, and the Monks of the Aether**.

#### 7. The Colossus Faction (*The Pillar-Mind*)
* **The Divine Faculty:** Immovable scale, tectonic patience, and planetary weight.
* **Subsequent Lineage:** The spirits that fused with the bedrock, giving birth to **The Five Slumbering Apex Dragons**—the living continental counterweights.

#### 8. The Voice Faction (*The Choral-Mind*)
* **The Divine Faculty:** Harmonic resonance, acoustic communion, and the vibration that binds disparate atoms into music.
* **Subsequent Lineage:** Ancestors of **First Cantor Sophia and the Sunken Patriarchate of Ostraka**.

#### 9. The Sovereign Crown (*The Imperial Will*)
* **The Divine Faculty:** The imperative of unity, governance, hierarchy, and the preservation of the central flame.
* **Subsequent Lineage:** The imperial line of **House Valerius**—kings cursed with the desperate obsession of keeping the fragments bound under one throne.

## CHAPTER III: THE GREAT INDIVIDUATION & THE SOWING OF LIFE

As eons passed across the higher dimensional axes, the nine factions began to pull apart. 

What began as complementary aspects of a single psyche evolved into distinct identities. The Armoured Faction grew proud of its invulnerability, looking down upon the wild unpredictability of the Beast. The Hammer Faction mocked the Scholars as idle dreamers who built nothing with their hands. The Jaw grew impatient with the slow, deliberate melodies of the Voice.

Each faction began to fashion mortal life in its own image:
* The Hammer forged **men of brass and piston**, breathing steam into clockwork ribcages.
* The Beast gave birth to **the horned broods of the forest**, whose bones were entwined with living roots.
* The Armoured created **the iron-blooded soldiers of the frontier**, whose marrow was dense as lead.
* The Scholars cultivated **the ascetics of the high crags**, who could live for years on thin air and starlight.

The world was vast, populous, and vibrant, but the pristine, singular harmony of the Supreme Light was thinning. Where once there was one voice singing in ten dimensions, there were now nine distinct choruses, each singing in a different key, each trying to drown out the others.

## CHAPTER IV: THE UNQUENCHABLE THIRST & THE CORRUPTION OF THE FLAME

Here lies the tragic flaw at the center of all created things: **Separation brings longing, and desperate longing turns into greed.**

Because each faction possessed only one-ninth of the divine mind, each felt an agonizing, hollow ache within its soul—a gnawing, existential emptiness that scholars later called **The Undying Thirst**.
* The Hammer felt empty without the biological warmth of the Beast.
* The Beast felt terrified and chaotic without the disciplined armor of the Aegis.
* The Armoured felt blind without the searching curiosity of the Explorer.
* The Scholars felt powerless without the crushing force of the Jaw.

Instead of reuniting in humility, the deities—the supreme, living avatars of each faction—concluded that the only way to heal their incompleteness was to **consume the others**.

They began to covet the central reservoir of the founding light: **The Hearth of the Universe**.

The deities believed that whoever absorbed the Hearth in its entirety would become the new, sole God of existence, swallowing their siblings and ending the torment of their incompleteness.

The Light was corrupted. What had been a beacon of magnanimity and unconditional love was twisted into a violent, competitive furnace. The factions forged reality-bending weapons:
* The Hammer built dimensional drills that punctured the fabric of space.
* The Jaw tore tectonic plates free from the continental mantle.
* The Scholars cast spells that unknitted mathematical constants.
* The Sovereign Crown sought to chain all nine deities to the imperial throne with leylines of molten gold.

The wars of the deities shook the foundations of the cosmos. Stars went out; entire dimensional axes began to warp, fray, and snap under the strain of their titanic fury.

## CHAPTER V: THE NIGHT OF THE FALL (THE COLLAPSE INTO THREE DIMENSIONS)

Historians and chroniclers of later ages argue endlessly over the timeline of the ruin:
* *Could an empire that stood for ten thousand years collapse in a single evening?*
* *Could the paradise of the gods be reduced to ash between sunset and dawn?*

Many conservative scholars insist the Fall must have taken three centuries of gradual decay, masked by the frantic propaganda of the dying courts.

**The horrific reality, preserved only in the deepest archaeological ruins, is that the Fall occurred in a single, terrifying night.**

### The Dimensional Collapse (The Cosmic Foil)
When the conflict reached its frenzied climax at the central sanctuary, all nine factions unleashed their supreme power simultaneously upon the Resonance Monolith.

The Monolith did not merely break; its structural failure triggered a **catastrophic dimensional reduction cascade**—a localized cosmic collapse:
1. The **Tenth, Ninth, Eighth, Seventh, Sixth, Fifth, and Fourth Dimensions** folded inward upon themselves like an accordion of burning paper.
2. The boundless, infinite vistas of the Abode of Will were violently crushed and flattened into the narrow, suffocating confines of **Three Spatial Dimensions and One Direction of Time**.
3. Billions of living beings whose bodies existed across higher planes were instantly severed from their higher-dimensional mass. Their flesh mutated, their internal organs were crushed by the sudden imposition of raw gravity, and their minds were shattered by the instantaneous loss of their cosmic senses.
4. The oceans drained into newly opened subterranean cracks. The sky burned purple and black as the Celestial Ether was ripped away into an unreachable atmospheric ceiling. The vast deserts flash-melted into jagged black glass.

By morning, the sun did not rise over a glorious ten-dimensional paradise. It rose over a **bruised, bleeding, finite continent of rock, mud, and ash**.

The gods were dead. Their colossal corpses fell across the landscape, their divine marrow leaking into the soil like radioactive poison. The nine factions were reduced to shattered, traumatized tribes clinging to the ruins of their former greatness.

The world was christened **Ashenfall**—the place where the divine fire fell to earth and turned to ash.

Centuries have passed since that cataclysmic night.

The land of Ashenfall is vast, yet it is **strictly finite**—a scarred, continental pocket preserved inside an unravelled void. Beyond its borders lie no foreign lands, only **The Veil of Salt**: the ragged, misty edge where three-dimensional space ends and the unmade nothingness begins.

The world is trapped in a state of eternal, agonizing decay:
* **The Wrath of Dead Gods:** Though the nine deities are dead, their supreme essences cannot fully rot away. Their residual psychic radiation pulses through the earth:
  * In the **Cogwork March**, Otto Vance siphons the lingering bio-electricity of a dying storm god to turn the gears of screaming brass automatons.
  * In the **Gilded Dunes**, the molten blood of the Jaw god calcifies the bones of enslaved miners into indestructible netherite slag.
  * In the **Whispering Fen**, the decaying brain of the Beast deity feeds an eternal fungal hive-mind that whispers through the roots of weeping trees.
  * In the deep tectonic trenches, the **Three Slumbering Apex Dragons** twitch in their sleep, tormented by nightmares of the higher dimensions they can never again touch.

Yet despite the horror, **life continues**.

Mortal men and women farm the poisoned ash; knights maintain vigil on crumbling coastal bastions; artificers weld brass armor to fragile chests; and hermits freeze themselves on glacial summits seeking one final moment of quiet. They are tortured by the ghosts of a paradise they never saw, driven by a phantom instinct to piece together the shattered puzzle of their origins.

## CHAPTER VII: THE TENTH SCION (THE CARRIER OF THE LOST DIMENSION)

And now, upon the cold, black gravel of the Forgotten Shore, the tide washes a solitary body ashore.

You awaken with salt in your mouth, water in your lungs, and **Waterlogged Boots** locked to your feet.

You are the **Tenth Scion**—the forgotten child born during the final eclipse before the Fall. In your veins does not flow the corrupted, one-ninth blood of the warring clans. 

Within your heart burns the **Tenth Dimension**—the uncorrupted, singular core of the founding light that was cast into the abyss before the nine deities tore each other to pieces.

* This is why you possess **20 Hearts (40 Max HP)**: your physical form is anchored to the lost higher dimension, allowing your mortal flesh to withstand kinetic shocks that would pulverize ordinary men.
* This is why the relics of the dead gods react violently to your touch.
* This is why the needle of the **Pilgrim's Compass** twitches toward your chest.

You are not merely an exile seeking survival. You are the final surviving fragment of the **Abode of Will**, walking through the ruins of your broken nursery to decide whether this scarred, finite world should be reignited in tyrannical gold, put to sleep beneath the silent waves, mechanized in endless brass, or permanently liberated into the honest dawn of mortal man.
